Privacy Policy

At RoadReady JA, your privacy is our priority. We are committed to transparency and to ensuring that your personal information is protected while providing you with an innovative digital experience.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as your name, email address, Tax Registration Number (TRN), and other specific details strictly necessary to process your driver's licence applications, renewals, or verifications.

Your information is used securely and solely for its intended purpose: to authenticate your identity, process driver's licence documentation via government servers, communicate critical updates regarding your application status, and provide prompt customer support.

Data Security

We deploy robust, industry-standard security protocols—including end-to-end encryption—to protect your personal and sensitive data against unauthorized access, accidental alteration, disclosure, or any other forms of malicious compromise.

Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, your rights, or our data handling practices, please do not hesitate to contact our team directly through the contact form on our home page.
